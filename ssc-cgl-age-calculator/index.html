How Is Age Calculated for SSC CGL?

Determining age eligibility for the Staff Selection Commission Combined Graduate Level Examination is not a matter of simple subtraction between your birth year and the exam year. The Commission enforces strict statutory benchmarks laid down in the official notification. Here is the five-step legal methodology used to determine candidate eligibility:

Step 01

Matriculation DOB Baseline

The Commission recognizes only the date of birth recorded in your Secondary Examination / 10th Class Passing Certificate. Birth certificates issued by municipal bodies, school leaving certificates, or Aadhaar cards are not admissible if they conflict with the 10th marksheet.

Step 02

Crucial Reckoning Date

Under Department of Personnel and Training (DoPT) guidelines, examinations held in the second half of the calendar year anchor age reckoning to 1 August of that year. All calculations are executed precisely as on 01-08-2026, regardless of your application date.

Step 03

Calendar Difference Computation

Your age is computed in completed years, months, and exact days as of 1 August 2026. A candidate born on 2 August 1999 has not yet celebrated their birthday on 1 August, and therefore remains 26 years old for the 18–27 band calculation.

Step 04

Post-Specific Band Matching

Because SSC CGL combines Group 'B' Gazetted, Group 'B' Non-Gazetted, and Group 'C' posts into one test, the age limits diverge into four distinct brackets: 18–27, 20–30, 18–30, and 18–32. Each post cadre must be evaluated individually.

Step 05

Statutory Relaxation Stacking

Upper-age concessions (OBC +3y, SC/ST +5y, PwBD +10y, ESM service deductions) are appended to the upper limit of the target post. However, minimum age requirements (18 or 20 years) are strictly non-relaxable across all categories.

Category-Wise Age Relaxation Rules

Statutory reservation confers upper-age concessions in accordance with Government of India orders. Below is the authoritative breakdown of upper-age extensions and qualifying conditions:

+3 YEARS

OBC (Non-Creamy Layer)

Applies to candidates belonging to Central List OBC castes holding a valid Non-Creamy Layer certificate issued within the crucial validity window. For 18–30 posts, upper age becomes 33 years; for 18–27 posts, it becomes 30 years.

+5 YEARS

Scheduled Castes & Tribes (SC / ST)

Confers 5 years extension over the post's standard ceiling. For 18–30 posts, maximum age becomes 35 years; for JSO (18–32), maximum age becomes 37 years. Applicable to all Group B and C cadres.

+10 TO +15 YEARS

Persons with Benchmark Disabilities (PwBD)

PwBD (Unreserved/EWS) receives +10 years. PwBD + OBC receives +13 years cumulative. PwBD + SC/ST receives +15 years cumulative. Must possess a valid disability certificate of 40% or higher.

SERVICE DEDUCTION + 3 YRS

Ex-Servicemen (ESM)

Computed by deducting the period of actual military service rendered from the candidate's actual age on closing date. If the adjusted age exceeds the post ceiling by ≤ 3 years, the veteran qualifies.

UP TO 40 / 45 YEARS

Central Govt Civilian Employees

Civilian employees who have rendered not less than 3 years regular and continuous service as on crucial date can apply up to 40 years of age for Group 'C' posts (45 years for SC/ST).

NO RELAXATION

EWS (Economically Weaker)

Under official central norms, EWS candidates are granted 10% reservation in vacancies, but no concession in age limit. EWS candidates must meet the same age criteria as Unreserved (General) applicants.

Candidate Age: 28 Years (UR)

Ineligible for Band 1 (Auditors, Tax Assistants at 18–27). However, fully eligible for Band 2 (ASO CSS, MEA at 20–30), Band 3 (Excise/IT Inspector at 18–30), and Band 4 (JSO at 18–32).

Candidate Age: 33 Years (OBC)

Status: Category-Relaxed Eligibility

As a General candidate, they would be overage for all posts except JSO. But with +3 years OBC relaxation, their ceiling for 18–30 posts expands to 33 years, keeping IT Inspector and Excise Inspector completely open.

Which Date of Birth Is Accepted for SSC CGL?

A surprising number of candidates face cancellation of candidature during Document Verification (DV) due to DOB discrepancies between their application and physical certificates. Here are the Commission's non-negotiable rules:

The Supreme Authority: 10th / Secondary Certificate

The Staff Selection Commission operates under a rigid legal framework: Only the Date of Birth recorded in the Matriculation / Secondary Examination Certificate or an equivalent certificate issued by a recognized Board is accepted.

  • Municipal Birth Certificates: Not accepted if they display a different date than your 10th marksheet.
  • Aadhaar Card / Voter ID / Passport: These are photo identity proofs, NOT legal age proofs for SSC examination entry. If your Aadhaar shows 12-07-1999 and your 10th marksheet shows 12-08-1999, the Commission will enforce 12-08-1999.
  • Affidavits: Affidavits claiming that the school recorded the wrong birthdate are rejected outright by the Commission.
  • Formal Gazette Corrections: If your board corrected your birthdate, the formal corrigendum or re-issued certificate must bear an issuance date prior to the crucial closing date of the application.

Common SSC CGL Age Calculation Mistakes

Avoid these six critical traps that frequently lead candidates to incorrectly disqualify themselves or submit invalid applications:

Mistake 01: Using Today's Date

Calculating your age as of today rather than the crucial date (1 August 2026). If you turn 28 in October 2026, you are still 27 on 1 August and eligible for 18–27 posts!

Mistake 02: Assuming 32 Is Universal

Reading "Age Limit up to 32 Years" on third-party websites and assuming all posts permit 32. The 32-year limit applies exclusively to JSO; most executive posts cap at 27 or 30.

Mistake 03: Assuming EWS Gets Age Concession

EWS candidates receive vacancy quota, not age relaxation. EWS candidates aged 30+ cannot apply for 18–30 posts without another qualifying relaxation (such as PwBD).

Mistake 04: Confusing Minimum Age Requirements

Assistant Section Officer in CSS, MEA, Railways and Sub Inspector in CBI require a minimum age of 20 years on 1 August. An 18-year-old graduate is ineligible for these posts.

Mistake 05: Creamy Layer OBC Misconception

OBC candidates whose family income exceeds the Creamy Layer threshold are treated as General / Unreserved for age purposes and receive no 3-year relaxation.

Mistake 06: Relying on Online Tools Without Notice Verification

Using generic age tools that ignore the 02-August cutoff boundaries. The official notice specifies "born not earlier than 02-08-1996" rather than a blanket year subtraction.

What If My Birthday Is After August 1?

One of the most frequent dilemmas aspirants face involves birthdays in late August, September, October, November, or December. Because the crucial date is anchored to 1 August 2026, your birthday has not yet occurred for the reckoning:

Worked Example: Candidate Born on 15 August 1998

  • On 15 August 2026, the candidate will turn 28 years old.
  • However, on the crucial date of 1 August 2026, they are exactly 27 years, 11 months, and 17 days old.
  • Verdict: Under the law, they have not yet crossed 28. For posts requiring 20–30 or 18–30 years, they are comfortably within the limit!

Frequently Asked Questions

Get authoritative answers to the most common queries regarding SSC CGL age criteria, cutoff dates, and relaxations:

What is the age reckoning date for SSC CGL 2026?
For SSC CGL 2026, the crucial date for age reckoning is 1 August 2026 (01-08-2026). A candidate's age must fall within the prescribed limits on this exact cutoff date regardless of when the notification was released or when you submit your application form.
What are the age limits for various posts in SSC CGL 2026?
SSC CGL features four major post-specific age bands:
  • 18–27 Years: Auditors (C&AG, CGA), Accountants, Tax Assistants (CBDT, CBIC), UDC/SSA.
  • 20–30 Years: Assistant Section Officers (CSS, MEA, Railways, AFHQ), Sub-Inspector in CBI.
  • 18–30 Years: Inspector of Income Tax, Inspector (Central Excise), Preventive Officer, Examiner, AEO in Enforcement Directorate.
  • 18–32 Years: Junior Statistical Officer (JSO) in MoSPI.
Which Date of Birth document is accepted by the Staff Selection Commission?
The Commission strictly accepts only the Date of Birth recorded in the Matriculation or Secondary Examination Certificate. Aadhaar cards, school leaving certificates, affidavits, or municipal certificates with different dates are not accepted.
What is the age relaxation for OBC candidates in SSC CGL?
OBC (Non-Creamy Layer) candidates receive an upper-age relaxation of 3 years over the post's standard maximum limit. For an 18–30 post, the effective upper age limit becomes 33 years; for an 18–27 post, it becomes 30 years.
What is the age relaxation for SC and ST candidates in SSC CGL?
SC and ST candidates receive an upper-age relaxation of 5 years over the prescribed upper limit. For an 18–30 post, the maximum age limit becomes 35 years; for an 18–32 post, it becomes 37 years.
Can I apply for SSC CGL if I am 31 years old?
Yes, depending on your category and the post. As an Unreserved candidate, you are eligible for Junior Statistical Officer (JSO, 18–32 band). If you belong to OBC, SC, ST, PwBD, or Ex-Servicemen categories, your upper age ceiling is extended, making 18–30 and 20–30 posts accessible as well.
Does EWS category get age relaxation in SSC CGL?
No. Under Government of India rules, EWS (Economically Weaker Sections) candidates receive 10% reservation in vacancies, but no concession in upper age limit. EWS candidates must meet the same age criteria as Unreserved applicants.
How is Ex-Servicemen (ESM) age calculated in SSC CGL?
For Ex-Servicemen, the actual period of military service rendered is deducted from the candidate's actual age as on the closing date. If the adjusted resultant age does not exceed the prescribed maximum age limit by more than 3 years, the candidate is considered age-eligible.
What if my birthday falls after 1 August in 2026?
Because age is determined strictly as of 1 August 2026, your birthday later in the year has not yet occurred as of the crucial date. For example, if you were born on 15 August 1998, on 1 August 2026 you are still 27 years old, keeping you eligible for 18–30 and 20–30 posts.
Is there an attempt limit for SSC CGL?
No. Unlike the UPSC Civil Services Examination, SSC CGL currently imposes no limit on the number of attempts. You may appear as many times as you wish provided you satisfy the age and educational criteria on the crucial date.
