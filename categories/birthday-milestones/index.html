How a Birthday Countdown Actually Works

Build this year's birthday first, then advance a year only if it has already gone — that single rule handles every case including the day itself.

Birthday & Milestones

What a Golden Birthday Is, and When Yours Falls

Your golden birthday is the one where your age matches the day of the month you were born — turning 18 on the 18th.

Birthday & Milestones

Half Birthdays: Six Months, Not 182 Days

A half birthday is six calendar months after your birthday, which is somewhere between 181 and 184 days depending on where it falls.

Birthday & Milestones

When Will You Be 10,000 Days Old?

Ten thousand days is about 27 years and 4 months — and unlike a birthday, the exact date requires actual counting.

Birthday & Milestones

Your Billion-Second Birthday

One billion seconds is 11,574 days — about 31 years and 8 months — and the exact moment depends on a birth time most people do not have.

Birthday & Milestones

Planning a Milestone Birthday Date

A milestone birthday is a calendar anniversary, so it is found by adding whole years to the birth date — not by adding days.
