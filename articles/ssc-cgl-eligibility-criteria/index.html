SSC CGL Eligibility Criteria 2026: Age, Qualification, Nationality & More

To apply for the Combined Graduate Level (CGL) examination, candidates must satisfy the statutory eligibility conditions specified in the official Staff Selection Commission notification. These mandates encompass your age on the reckoning date, essential educational qualifications, nationality or citizenship status, post-specific requirements, and valid category documentation.

Unlike single-post exams, SSC CGL evaluates candidates across a multi-stage sequential checklist. Being eligible for one vacancy does not automatically guarantee eligibility across all 30+ ministerial cadres. Review the complete statutory checklist, cutoff dates, and interactive eligibility evaluation tools below.

SSC CGL Eligibility Criteria 2026 at a Glance

The Staff Selection Commission establishes mandatory statutory eligibility conditions across seven distinct dimensions. Every candidate must satisfy all conditions applicable to their chosen vacancy simultaneously on the prescribed reckoning date:

Eligibility Factor Statutory Mandate What Candidates Must Verify
Age Limit 18–27, 20–30, 18–30, or 18–32 Years Reckoned strictly as of 01 August 2026 based on the targeted post.
Date of Birth (DOB) Falls inside post-specific calendar window Must match the exact Date of Birth recorded on your 10th / Matriculation Certificate.
Educational Qualification Bachelor’s Degree or Equivalent Must be awarded by a UGC-recognized university on or before 01 August 2026.
Nationality / Citizenship Indian Citizen / Specified Categories Citizen of India, or subject of Nepal/Bhutan with Government of India certificate.
Post-Specific Criteria Specialized subjects or physical tests 60% Math in Class 12 for JSO; Physical endurance & height norms for CBIC/CBI/NIA.
Category Concessions Statutory upper-age relaxation OBC (+3), SC/ST (+5), PwBD (+10 to +15), ESM service deductions where applicable.
Required Documents Original verification certificates Valid certificates in official Central Government format presented at document verification.

1. SSC CGL Age Eligibility

A common misconception among aspirants is that SSC CGL operates under a single uniform age limit. In reality, SSC CGL does not have one universal age limit for every post. The statutory boundaries vary significantly across the administrative hierarchy, governed by departmental recruitment rules:

  • 18–27 Years (Group C Ministerial Cadres): Covers feeder line vacancies including Tax Assistant (CBDT and CBIC), Auditor (C&AG, CGDA, CGA), Accountant, and Upper Division Clerk (UDC).
  • 20–30 Years (Secretariat Group B Cadres): Requires an elevated entry floor of 20 years for Assistant Section Officer (ASO) posts in Central Secretariat Service (CSS), Ministry of External Affairs (MEA), Intelligence Bureau (IB), and Ministry of Railways.
  • 18–30 Years (Executive & Enforcement Cadres): Encompasses premier investigatory roles including Inspector of Central Excise, Inspector of Income Tax, Customs Preventive Officer, Customs Examiner, and Assistant Enforcement Officer (ED).
  • 18–32 Years (Specialized Statistics Cadres): The highest base ceiling in the examination, applicable to the Junior Statistical Officer (JSO) in MoSPI and Statistical Investigator Grade-II in the Office of the Registrar General of India.

All candidate ages are reckoned strictly as on 01 August 2026. 2. SSC CGL Date of Birth Eligibility

Age eligibility in government examinations is not assessed in abstract whole numbers. Instead, the Staff Selection Commission converts statutory age brackets into precise calendar dates of birth:

The SSC CGL DOB Reckoning Chain:
Date of Birth → Age on 01 August 2026 → Target Cadre Bracket → Statutory Eligibility

For General (Unreserved) candidates, the official notification establishes the following permissible Date of Birth corridors:

  • 18–27 Years: Born not earlier than 02-08-1999 and not later than 01-08-2008.
  • 20–30 Years: Born not earlier than 02-08-1996 and not later than 01-08-2006.
  • 18–30 Years: Born not earlier than 02-08-1996 and not later than 01-08-2008.
  • 18–32 Years: Born not earlier than 02-08-1994 and not later than 01-08-2008.
Mandatory Source Proof: The Commission strictly recognizes the Date of Birth recorded in your Matriculation or Secondary Examination Certificate. Birth certificates issued by municipal bodies, hospital records, affidavits, or Aadhaar cards bearing conflicting dates are summarily rejected during Document Verification.

3. SSC CGL Educational Qualification

Because SSC CGL is the Combined Graduate Level examination, the foundational qualification across the board is a recognized university degree. However, educational requirements are not completely identical for all cadres. The official notification divides vacancies into general graduate posts and specialized technical posts:

Cadre Classification Target Posts Mandatory Educational Requirement
General Administrative & Clerical ASO (CSS, MEA, Railways), Tax Assistant, Auditor, Accountant, UDC, Executive Assistant Bachelor’s Degree in any discipline from a recognized University or equivalent qualification approved by the Central Government.
Junior Statistical Officer (JSO) Ministry of Statistics and Programme Implementation (MoSPI) Bachelor’s Degree in any subject with at least 60% Marks in Mathematics at 12th standard level;
OR
Bachelor’s Degree in any subject with Statistics as one of the subjects at degree level.
Statistical Investigator Gr-II Office of Registrar General of India (Ministry of Home Affairs) Bachelor’s Degree in any subject with Statistics as one of the subjects. The candidate must have studied Statistics in all the three years / all six semesters of the graduation course.
Research Assistant National Human Rights Commission (NHRC) Essential: Bachelor’s Degree from a recognized university.
Desirable: Minimum 1-year research experience in any recognized institution or Degree in Law / Human Rights.
Assistant Audit Officer / Accounts Officer (AAO) Indian Audit & Accounts Department (C&AG) Essential: Bachelor’s Degree from a recognized university.
Desirable: Chartered Accountant (CA), Cost & Management Accountant (CMA), CS, or Master of Commerce (M.Com) / MBA (Finance).

Candidates who earned degrees through distance education or open universities (such as IGNOU) are fully eligible, provided the university and programme are officially recognized by the University Grants Commission (UGC) and Distance Education Bureau (DEB).

Can Final-Year Students Apply for SSC CGL?

One of the most frequently asked questions by college seniors is whether they can participate in SSC CGL before their final university degree is awarded. The Staff Selection Commission addresses this scenario with strict statutory language:

The Crucial Educational Cutoff Rule: Candidates appearing in the final year of their graduation are eligible to apply, provided they acquire the essential educational qualification on or before 01 August 2026.

To evaluate whether you qualify as a final-year student, examine how the Commission audits academic qualifications:

  1. Date of Result Declaration Governs Eligibility: The decisive legal benchmark is not the date of convocation, nor the date when your physical degree certificate is printed months later. The crucial coordinate is the official date of result declaration printed on your final mark sheet or provisional certificate.
  2. Result Declared On or Before 01-Aug-2026: If your university officially publishes your final semester results on or before 01 August 2026, you are statutorily qualified, even if your physical degree parchment has not yet been collected.
  3. Result Declared After 01-Aug-2026: If your university declares your result on 02 August 2026 or later, you are legally disqualified. Merely appearing in the final examination or waiting for delayed university evaluations does not satisfy the requirement.
  4. Mandatory Documentary Proof: During Document Verification (DV), candidates must produce documentary proof (provisional certificate or mark sheet) demonstrating that the examination result was officially declared on or before the cutoff date.
Important Summary: Being enrolled in the final year of a graduation course is not the same as satisfying the educational requirement. Check with your university registrar to verify whether final results will be officially gazetted before 01 August 2026.

4. SSC CGL Nationality and Citizenship Eligibility

Under Para 5 of the official recruitment notification, a candidate must belong to one of the authorized citizenship or nationality categories recognized by the Government of India:

Category Statutory Eligibility Condition Documentary Proof Required
Citizen of India Natural-born or registered citizen of India under the Citizenship Act, 1955. Valid Indian passport, Voter ID, Aadhaar, or Birth Certificate.
Subject of Nepal Eligible under bilateral treaty arrangements between India and Nepal. Certificate of eligibility issued by the Government of India.
Subject of Bhutan Eligible under bilateral treaty arrangements between India and Bhutan. Certificate of eligibility issued by the Government of India.
Tibetan Refugee Must have entered India before 1st January 1962 with the intention of permanently settling in India. Certificate of eligibility issued by the Government of India / competent authority.
Person of Indian Origin (PIO) Migrated from Pakistan, Burma, Sri Lanka, or East African countries (Kenya, Uganda, Tanzania, Zambia, Malawi, Zaire, Ethiopia, Vietnam) with intention of permanent settlement. Certificate of eligibility issued by the Government of India.

Note on Certificate of Eligibility: Candidates belonging to categories other than Indian citizens may be admitted to the examination, but the offer of appointment will only be issued after the necessary eligibility certificate has been officially granted by the Government of India.

5. Do All SSC CGL Posts Have the Same Eligibility Requirements?

No. SSC CGL is an umbrella recruitment framework combining over 30 distinct Central Government posts. In addition to varying age limits and educational criteria, several enforcement and investigatory cadres enforce strict physical fitness standards and physical tests.

Physical Standards for CBIC Cadres (Excise Inspector, Preventive Officer, Examiner)

Candidates selecting posts under the Central Board of Indirect Taxes and Customs (CBIC) and Central Bureau of Narcotics (CBN) must satisfy mandatory physical benchmarks:

Gender Minimum Height Chest Measurement Physical Endurance Test
Male Candidates 157.5 cm (Relaxed by 5 cm for Gorkhas, Garhwalis, Assamese, and ST candidates) 81 cm fully expanded, with a minimum expansion of 5 cm Walking: 1,600 metres in 15 minutes
Cycling: 8 kilometres in 30 minutes
Female Candidates 152 cm (Relaxed by 2.5 cm for hill tribes and ST candidates) Weight: 48 kg (Relaxed by 2 kg for reserved groups) Walking: 1 kilometre in 20 minutes
Cycling: 3 kilometres in 25 minutes

Physical & Medical Standards for CBI & NIA Sub-Inspectors

  • Sub-Inspector in Central Bureau of Investigation (CBI): Height for men is 165 cm; for women 150 cm (relaxed by 5 cm for Hillsmen and Tribals). Chest is 76 cm with expansion. Distant vision must be 6/6 in one eye and 6/9 in the other with or without glasses.
  • Sub-Inspector in National Investigation Agency (NIA): Height for men is 170 cm; for women 150 cm (relaxed by 5 cm). Eye sight requires distant vision 6/6 and 6/9.

6. Category and Age Relaxation

If a candidate has exceeded the standard Unreserved age limit for their targeted vacancy, statutory upper-age relaxation may preserve their eligibility. The Staff Selection Commission applies concessions based on social category, disability status, and public service:

  • OBC (Non-Creamy Layer): +3 years added to the post maximum limit.
  • SC / ST Candidates: +5 years added to the post maximum limit.
  • PwBD (Benchmark Disability): +10 years (General), +13 years (OBC), +15 years (SC/ST).
  • Ex-Servicemen (ESM): 3 years relaxation after deducting completed military service from actual age on 01 August 2026.
  • Central Government Civilian Employees: Up to 40 years of age (General/OBC) and 45 years (SC/ST) for Group C vacancies after completing 3 years of regular continuous service.

Age relaxation modifies the maximum permissible age and shifts your earliest allowable Date of Birth backwards. It never lowers the statutory minimum entry age (18 or 20 years). Documents You Must Check Before Applying

Eligibility is only as solid as the official documentation verifying it. Claiming eligibility during online application without holding the prescribed certificates in the mandated format results in rejection during Document Verification (DV). Verify these five core document groups:

  1. Matriculation (10th Standard) Certificate: Serves as the ultimate statutory proof of name, father's name, and Date of Birth. Ensure the spelling matches your application exactly.
  2. Degree Certificate & Mark Sheets: Original graduation degree certificate or provisional certificate issued by the university registrar, clearly bearing the date of result declaration on or before 01 August 2026.
  3. Caste / Community Certificates (SC, ST, OBC-NCL, EWS): Must be issued by authorized revenue authorities (Tehsildar, Sub-Divisional Magistrate, or District Magistrate) strictly in the Central Government prescribed format. State-level formats without Central Government validity clauses are routinely rejected. For OBC-NCL and EWS, ensure the certificate reflects the valid financial year.
  4. Disability Certificate (for PwBD candidates): Certificate issued by a competent Medical Authority specifying a permanent disability of not less than 40% (benchmark disability) in the prescribed format.
  5. Discharge Book / Serving Certificate (for Ex-Servicemen): Record of service, discharge book, or NOC for defense personnel due for retirement within one year of the closing date.

The Multi-Condition SSC CGL Eligibility Formula

Statutory examination eligibility is a cumulative evaluation where failing a single variable invalidates the candidate's application:

AGE ON 01-AUG + MATRICULATION DOB + DEGREE ≤ 01-AUG + CITIZENSHIP + POST SPECIFICS + DOCUMENTS
= STATUTORY EXAMINATION ELIGIBILITY

Common SSC CGL Eligibility Mistakes to Avoid

Thousands of qualified applicants are disqualified annually due to easily preventable procedural errors during the eligibility audit. Guard against these six critical pitfalls:

1. Checking Only Age

Assuming that satisfying the age limit makes you automatically eligible without verifying graduation degree cutoff dates and physical requirements.

2. Assuming General Degree Qualifies for JSO

Applying for Junior Statistical Officer without having 60% in 12th Mathematics or Statistics as a graduation degree subject.

3. Ignoring Result Declaration Cutoff

Applying as a final-year student whose university issues examination results after 01 August 2026, leading to automatic cancellation.

4. Assuming Uniform Age Limits

Believing an age of 29 allows you to apply for all posts, when 18–27 Group C clerical posts (Tax Assistant, Auditor) are already closed for UR.

5. Using State Caste Certificates

Submitting state government format caste certificates that lack the required Government of India Non-Creamy Layer resolution numbers.

6. Overlooking CBIC Physical Standards

Selecting Inspector of Central Excise or Preventive Officer without verifying the mandatory 157.5 cm height and 1600m walking test requirements.

SSC CGL Eligibility Criteria 2026 FAQs

What is the eligibility criteria for SSC CGL 2026?
To apply for SSC CGL 2026, candidates must satisfy four foundational statutory conditions: (1) be a citizen of India (or eligible subject of Nepal/Bhutan), (2) hold a recognized Bachelor's Degree acquired on or before 01 August 2026, (3) fall within the prescribed age corridor (18–27, 20–30, 18–30, or 18–32 years) reckoned as of 01 August 2026, and (4) meet any post-specific educational or physical standards.
What qualification is required for SSC CGL?
The foundational educational qualification is a Bachelor's Degree in any discipline from a recognized university or institute. However, specific cadres have additional mandates: Junior Statistical Officer (JSO) requires either a Bachelor's Degree with Statistics or at least 60% marks in Mathematics at the Class 12 standard; Statistical Investigator Grade-II requires Statistics studied in all 3 years/all 6 semesters of graduation.
Is graduation compulsory for SSC CGL?
Yes. SSC CGL is the Combined Graduate Level Examination. A candidate must possess a completed Bachelor's Degree or equivalent recognized qualification. Candidates holding only higher secondary (10+2) or diploma certificates without an equivalent recognized graduation degree are not eligible.
Can final-year students apply for SSC CGL?
Yes, candidates appearing in their final year of graduation may apply, provided they acquire the essential educational qualification on or before the crucial cutoff date of 01 August 2026. The official result declaration date printed on the mark sheet or provisional certificate must be on or before 01-08-2026.
What is the age limit for SSC CGL?
SSC CGL does not have a single uniform age limit. Vacancies are grouped into four statutory age bands: 18–27 years for Group C clerical cadres, 20–30 years for Secretariat ASO cadres, 18–30 years for Executive Inspector cadres, and 18–32 years for Statistical Officers, reckoned as of 01 August 2026.
Is there age relaxation in SSC CGL?
Yes. Category candidates receive upper-age concessions beyond the post maximum: OBC receives +3 years, SC/ST receives +5 years, PwBD receives +10 to +15 years, and Ex-Servicemen receive 3 years of relaxation after deducting completed military service from their actual age.
Can OBC candidates get age relaxation in SSC CGL?
Yes. Candidates belonging to the Non-Creamy Layer (NCL) of Other Backward Classes receive 3 years of statutory upper-age relaxation, extending the ceiling to 30 years (for 18–27 posts), 33 years (for 18–30 and 20–30 posts), and 35 years (for 18–32 posts).
Can SC/ST candidates get age relaxation?
Yes. Scheduled Caste (SC) and Scheduled Tribe (ST) candidates receive 5 years of upper-age relaxation, allowing applications up to 32 years (for 18–27 posts), 35 years (for 18–30 and 20–30 posts), and 37 years (for 18–32 posts).
Are all SSC CGL posts available to every graduate?
No. While most general administrative and clerical posts (such as ASO in CSS, Tax Assistant, and Auditor) are open to any graduate, technical and enforcement posts have specific criteria. JSO requires mathematics/statistics qualifications, and Inspector posts in CBIC, CBI, and NIA enforce mandatory physical endurance and height standards.
Does SSC CGL have post-specific eligibility?
Yes. Individual posts carry distinct age floors (18 vs 20 years), age ceilings (27, 30, 32 years), specialized educational degrees, and physical fitness tests (1600m walking and 8km cycling for CBIC Inspector posts).
How can I check my SSC CGL eligibility?
You can verify your multi-factor eligibility using the interactive checklist on this page or the dedicated SSC CGL Age Calculator, which evaluates your exact Matriculation Date of Birth, graduation date, and category against all 30+ official recruitment cadres.
What documents are required for SSC CGL eligibility?
Candidates must possess: (1) Matriculation (10th) Certificate for proof of date of birth, (2) Bachelor's Degree certificate or provisional mark sheet with result declaration date ≤ 01-08-2026, (3) Caste/Category Certificate in the official Central Government format (for OBC/SC/ST/EWS), and (4) Valid photo identity proof (Aadhaar, Voter ID, or Passport).
