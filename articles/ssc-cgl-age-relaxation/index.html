SSC CGL Age Relaxation 2026: OBC, SC, ST, PwBD & Ex-Servicemen

The SSC CGL 2026 notification allows additional statutory upper-age relaxation for several eligible categories beyond the standard post-wise age limits. The exact benefit depends strictly on your social category, service status, targeted post cadre, and Department of Personnel and Training (DoPT) mandates.

If you have crossed the general age limit for a targeted vacancy, statutory relaxation may preserve your eligibility. All candidate ages and service deductions are reckoned strictly as of 01 August 2026. What Is Age Relaxation in SSC CGL?

In Indian civil service recruitment, age relaxation is a statutory concession that raises the maximum permissible age an applicant can attain on the official reckoning date (01 August 2026) without being disqualified as overage.

Rather than altering the entire examination cycle, the Commission attaches the relaxation directly to the upper ceiling of the applicant's chosen cadre.

Applicable Upper Age = Normal Post Age Limit + Applicable Relaxation

Subject strictly to the specific post cadre, valid category credentials, and DoPT notification provisions.

For example, consider an aspirant applying for the post of Inspector of Central Excise, which carries a statutory base limit of 18 to 30 years:

  • General (Unreserved) Candidate: Maximum permitted age is 30 years on 01 August 2026.
  • OBC Candidate (+3 Years): Maximum permitted age extends to 33 years (30 + 3).
  • SC/ST Candidate (+5 Years): Maximum permitted age extends to 35 years (30 + 5).
  • PwBD (UR) Candidate (+10 Years): Maximum permitted age extends to 40 years (30 + 10).

Crucially, age relaxation is unidirectional. It operates exclusively on the upper boundary. A 17-year-old candidate belonging to SC or OBC cannot claim relaxation to enter an 18-year post, because the statutory minimum age threshold can never be relaxed under any circumstance.

SSC CGL Age Relaxation for SC/ST Candidates

Candidates belonging to Scheduled Castes (SC) and Scheduled Tribes (ST) are granted a statutory concession of 5 years above the maximum age limit of any SSC CGL post they apply for.

The 5-year concession applies across all Group B executive and Group C clerical cadres:

  • 18–27 Cadres (Tax Assistant, Auditor, UDC): Permissible up to 32 years (27 + 5).
  • 20–30 Cadres (ASO in CSS, MEA, IB, Railway): Permissible up to 35 years (30 + 5). Minimum entry age of 20 still applies.
  • 18–30 Cadres (Inspectors in Central Excise, IT, Preventive Officer): Permissible up to 35 years (30 + 5).
  • 18–32 Cadres (Junior Statistical Officer): Permissible up to 37 years (32 + 5).
SC / ST Documentation Compliance

Caste Certificate Validity

To claim the 5-year concession, the caste certificate must be issued by a competent revenue authority (Tehsildar, Sub-Divisional Magistrate, or District Magistrate) in the prescribed Central Government format. State-level certificates without Central list recognition are rejected during Document Verification (DV).

SSC CGL Age Relaxation for OBC Candidates

Under DoPT notifications, candidates belonging to the Other Backward Classes (OBC) category receive an upper-age concession of 3 years.

Post-wise ceilings for OBC applicants:

  • 18–27 Group C Roles: Extended up to 30 years (27 + 3).
  • 20–30 Group B Officer Roles: Extended up to 33 years (30 + 3).
  • 18–30 Inspector Roles: Extended up to 33 years (30 + 3).
  • 18–32 JSO Cadre: Extended up to 35 years (32 + 3).
Crucial Check Before Claiming OBC Relaxation: Non-Creamy Layer (NCL) Status

OBC age relaxation is strictly reserved for candidates belonging to the Non-Creamy Layer (NCL). Candidates falling in the Creamy Layer are treated on par with General (Unreserved) applicants and receive zero age relaxation. Furthermore, the OBC certificate must be issued within the crucial 3-year validity window prior to the closing date of the application form.

SSC CGL Age Relaxation for PwBD Candidates

Persons with Benchmark Disabilities (PwBD)—defined as individuals with not less than 40% certified disability—receive the highest baseline age relaxations under Central Government norms.

The Staff Selection Commission explicitly categorizes PwBD relaxations into three distinct tiers:

PwBD (Unreserved) +10 Years

Permissible up to 40 years for 18–30 posts, and 42 years for JSO posts.

PwBD + OBC (NCL) +13 Years

Statutory combined band: 10 yrs PwBD + 3 yrs OBC. Permissible up to 43 years for 18–30 posts.

PwBD + SC / ST +15 Years

Statutory combined band: 10 yrs PwBD + 5 yrs SC/ST. Permissible up to 45 years for 18–30 posts.

PwBD candidates must also ensure that the specific post they choose is identified as suitable for their disability sub-category (e.g. Visual Impairment [VH], Hearing Impairment [HH], or Locomotor Disability [OH]) in the official vacancy matrix.

SSC CGL Age Relaxation for Ex-Servicemen (ESM)

A common misunderstanding among defense veterans is assuming that ESM relaxation simply means "add 3 years to your age." That is mathematically incorrect and leads to invalid applications.

The official SSC CGL notice defines ESM relaxation using a statutory net age deduction formula:

Adjusted Age = (Actual Age on 01-Aug-2026) − (Military Service Rendered)

The Adjusted Age must not exceed the post upper limit by more than 3 years.

Illustrative ESM Calculation Example

Consider an Ex-Serviceman applying for an 18–30 Inspector post:

  • Candidate's Actual Age on 01 August 2026: 37 Years
  • Regular Military Service Completed: 10 Years
  • Net Adjusted Age: 37 − 10 = 27 Years
  • Post Upper Limit: 30 Years
  • Statutory Evaluation: The adjusted age of 27 is well within the 30-year limit (in fact, it has not even touched the additional 3-year grace). The candidate is 100% Eligible!

Age Relaxation for Certain Disabled Defence Personnel

The Staff Selection Commission notification contains a specialized relaxation clause for defence personnel disabled during operational service:

  • General / OBC Defence Personnel: Concession of 3 years for personnel disabled in operations during hostilities with any foreign country or in a disturbed area and released as a consequence thereof.
  • SC / ST Defence Personnel: Concession of 8 years (3 years operational concession + 5 years SC/ST statutory allowance).

This provision requires documentary verification of disability discharge attributable directly to operational field deployment as authenticated by the competent military medical board.

SSC CGL Age Relaxation for Central Government Employees

Serving Central Government civilian employees who seek career advancement through SSC CGL can claim generous age concessions under Department of Personnel and Training rules, provided they fulfill two mandatory prerequisites:

  1. They must have rendered not less than 3 years of regular and continuous service under the Central Government as of the crucial closing date.
  2. The concession applies exclusively to Group C clerical and executive posts (such as Tax Assistant, Auditor, and Accountant). It does not apply to direct Group B gazetted or non-gazetted roles.

SC / ST Civilian Employees Up to 45 Years of Age 3 Years regular continuous service

Applicants must obtain and submit a formal 'No Objection Certificate' (NOC) and Service Certificate from their Head of Office during document scrutiny.

Can SSC CGL Age Relaxations Be Combined?

A frequent point of confusion among candidates with multiple eligibility claims (e.g. an OBC candidate who is also an Ex-Serviceman or Central Government Employee) is whether relaxations can be added cumulatively.

The rule is strict: You cannot manually combine or stack multiple relaxations. You can only claim the benefit of one single statutory category code as codified by the Commission.

✕ PROHIBITED MANUAL STACKING

Adding SC (+5) + OBC (+3) or adding ESM (+3) + Central Govt Employee (+10) manually. The application portal will automatically flag conflicting codes.

✓ STATUTORY COMBINED BANDS

Claiming officially gazetted combined codes: PwBD + OBC (Code 05 → 13 Years) or PwBD + SC/ST (Code 06 → 15 Years).

How Age Relaxation Changes Your Eligible Date of Birth

When the Commission grants upper-age relaxation, it directly alters your permissible Date of Birth window by shifting the earliest permissible birth date backwards into earlier calendar years.

For example, in the standard 18–30 Inspector cadre with an age reckoning date of 01 August 2026:

  • General (UR) Candidates: Earliest permissible DOB is 02 August 1996 (Must not be older than 30).
  • OBC (+3 Years): Earliest permissible DOB shifts 3 years earlier to 02 August 1993.
  • SC / ST (+5 Years): Earliest permissible DOB shifts 5 years earlier to 02 August 1991.
  • PwBD (UR, +10 Years): Earliest permissible DOB shifts to 02 August 1986.

However, the latest permissible birth date never shifts. For an 18-year post, the latest allowed DOB is always 01 August 2008 regardless of reservation status.

Common SSC CGL Age Relaxation Mistakes

Avoid these six frequent procedural errors that cause application rejection or post-selection cancellation during scrutiny:

MISTAKE 01
Using Today's Age Instead of Crucial Date

Calculating relaxation using today's calendar date rather than the fixed statutory date of 01 August 2026.

MISTAKE 02
Assuming Every Post Has the Same Base Ceiling

Assuming OBC means 33 years for every vacancy. For 18–27 posts (Tax Assistant), 27 + 3 = 30 years only.

MISTAKE 03
Manual Cumulative Stacking

Attempting to add SC (+5) and ESM (+3) together manually instead of claiming a single codified notification category.

MISTAKE 04
Assuming OBC Automatically Means Relaxation

Candidates in the Creamy Layer receive zero relaxation. A valid Non-Creamy Layer (NCL) certificate is mandatory.

MISTAKE 05
Treating ESM as a Simple +3 Addition

Failing to deduct military service rendered from actual age before evaluating against the post maximum threshold.

MISTAKE 06
Neglecting Post-Specific Eligibility Criteria

Qualifying for age relaxation but lacking essential educational requirements (such as 60% Mathematics/Statistics for JSO).

SSC CGL Age Relaxation 2026 FAQs

Does SSC CGL have age relaxation?
Yes. The Staff Selection Commission provides statutory upper-age relaxation beyond the prescribed post limit for candidates belonging to reserved categories (SC, ST, OBC, PwBD, Ex-Servicemen, and eligible Central Government Civilian Employees) reckoned as of 01 August 2026.
How much age relaxation is available for OBC in SSC CGL?
Eligible Non-Creamy Layer (NCL) OBC candidates receive a statutory upper-age relaxation of 3 years above the maximum age limit of the targeted post. For example, for an 18–30 Inspector post, an OBC candidate can apply up to 33 years of age.
How much age relaxation is available for SC/ST candidates?
Scheduled Caste (SC) and Scheduled Tribe (ST) candidates receive a statutory upper-age relaxation of 5 years above the prescribed post maximum limit. For an 18–30 post, the effective upper age becomes 35 years; for an 18–32 post, it becomes 37 years.
What is the SSC CGL age relaxation for PwBD candidates?
Unreserved (General) PwBD candidates with benchmark physical disability receive 10 years of statutory upper-age relaxation above the prescribed post limit.
What is the age relaxation for PwBD OBC?
Candidates belonging to PwBD + OBC (Non-Creamy Layer) receive an official combined statutory upper-age relaxation of 13 years (10 years PwBD + 3 years OBC) as explicitly codified in the SSC notification.
What is the age relaxation for PwBD SC/ST?
Candidates belonging to PwBD + SC/ST receive an official combined statutory upper-age relaxation of 15 years (10 years PwBD + 5 years SC/ST) above the maximum age limit of the targeted post.
Do ex-servicemen get age relaxation in SSC CGL?
Yes. Ex-Servicemen (ESM) receive a statutory relaxation of 3 years after deducting the exact period of military service rendered from their actual age as on 01 August 2026.
How is ESM age relaxation calculated?
ESM relaxation uses a statutory mathematical deduction: Actual Age on 01 August 2026 minus completed Military Service rendered. The resulting adjusted age must not exceed the prescribed post maximum age limit by more than 3 years.
Can age relaxation make me eligible for an older-age post?
Age relaxation extends the upper-age boundary for posts you are otherwise overage for, provided you satisfy the educational and physical criteria of that cadre. However, age relaxation never lowers the minimum entry age (18 or 20 years).
Can I combine different SSC CGL age relaxations?
No. You cannot arbitrarily add multiple relaxations together (such as SC + OBC). You can only claim the specific, singular combined category code officially defined by the Staff Selection Commission, such as PwBD + OBC (13 years) or PwBD + SC/ST (15 years).
Does age relaxation apply to every SSC CGL post?
Age relaxation applies across posts where reservation is statutorily permissible, but the resulting upper-age ceiling depends directly on the base limit of that specific post (e.g. 27 + 3 = 30 for Tax Assistant; 30 + 3 = 33 for Excise Inspector).
What is the age limit for Central Government employees in Group C posts?
Central Government Civilian Employees who have rendered not less than 3 years of regular and continuous service can apply for eligible Group C posts up to 40 years of age for General/OBC candidates, and up to 45 years of age for SC/ST candidates.
