SSC CGL Post-Wise Age Limit 2026: Age Limit for Every Post

SSC CGL posts do not all share the same age limit. Depending on the targeted cadre, recruitment rules classify vacancies into four distinct statutory age bands: 18–27, 20–30, 18–30, and 18–32 years. Checking the exact age requirement for your specific post code is essential before evaluating your eligibility.

Why Is the SSC CGL Age Limit Different for Different Posts?

A common source of confusion among first-time applicants is the assumption that because SSC CGL is a single Tier-I and Tier-II exam, there must be a single overarching age ceiling.

In administrative law, the Staff Selection Commission does not create the qualification norms. It acts as an examining body for over 25 distinct cadre-controlling authorities (such as the Central Board of Indirect Taxes, Ministry of External Affairs, and the Comptroller & Auditor General).

Step 1 One Examination SSC CGL Combined Exam
Step 2 Multiple Ministries Separate Cadre Authorities
Step 3 Distinct Service Rules Gazetted Recruitment Acts
Step 4 4 Age Bands 18–27, 20–30, 18–30, 18–32

For example, Section Officers in the MEA or CSS undergo extensive diplomatic and administrative training, leading the DoPT to mandate a minimum entry age of 20 years. Conversely, field-level Tax Assistants in direct taxes are recruited under clerical recruitment rules with an upper ceiling of 27 years.

Matching Post Cadres

SSC CGL Posts With 18–27 Age Limit

The 18–27 age bracket encompasses clerical, ministerial, and accounts support roles classified under Pay Level 4 and Level 5. These cadres represent a significant portion of the total vacancies:

  • Auditor: Offices under Comptroller & Auditor General of India (C&AG), Controller General of Accounts (CGA), and Controller General of Defence Accounts (CGDA).
  • Tax Assistant: Central Board of Direct Taxes (CBDT) and Central Board of Indirect Taxes & Customs (CBIC).
  • Accountant / Junior Accountant: Department of Post, Ministry of Telecommunications, and Controller General of Accounts.
  • Upper Division Clerk (UDC) / Senior Administrative Assistant: Department of Water Resources, Ministry of Textiles, and Military Engineer Services (MES).

Statutory Boundary (General Category): Born not earlier than 02 August 1999 and not later than 01 August 2008.

SSC CGL Posts With 20–30 Age Limit

The 20–30 age bracket is reserved exclusively for Group B Assistant Section Officer (ASO) positions in high-profile Central Government ministries and secretariats (Pay Level 7):

  • ASO in Central Secretariat Service (CSS): DoPT cadre responsible for policy drafting and inter-ministerial administration.
  • ASO in Ministry of External Affairs (MEA): Cadre offering diplomatic foreign postings and consular administration.
  • ASO in Intelligence Bureau (IB): Ministry of Home Affairs intelligence desk operations.
  • ASO in Ministry of Railways: Railway Board secretarial service.
  • ASO in Armed Forces Headquarters (AFHQ): Defence civil service administration.

Statutory Boundary (General Category): Born not earlier than 02 August 1996 and not later than 01 August 2006. Candidates aged 18 or 19 are strictly underage for these cadres.

SSC CGL Posts With 18–30 Age Limit

The 18–30 age bracket comprises the premier executive and enforcement roles of the Indian Revenue Service support structure (Pay Level 7):

  • Inspector of Central Excise / CGST: Central Board of Indirect Taxes & Customs (CBIC).
  • Inspector (Preventive Officer): Port and airport customs intelligence and antismuggling.
  • Inspector (Examiner): Cargo assessment and customs appraisal at major ports.
  • Inspector of Income Tax: Central Board of Direct Taxes (CBDT).
  • Assistant Enforcement Officer (AEO): Directorate of Enforcement (ED), Department of Revenue.
  • Sub-Inspector: Central Bureau of Investigation (CBI) and National Investigation Agency (NIA).
  • Divisional Accountant: Offices under Comptroller and Auditor General of India (CAG) - Pay Level 6.

Statutory Boundary (General Category): Born not earlier than 02 August 1996 and not later than 01 August 2008.

SSC CGL Posts With 18–32 Age Limit

The 18–32 age bracket offers the highest base upper-age ceiling in the examination:

  • Junior Statistical Officer (JSO): Ministry of Statistics & Programme Implementation (MoSPI) - Pay Level 6. Requires a Bachelor's Degree with at least 60% in Mathematics at 12th standard or Statistics as a degree subject.
  • Statistical Investigator Grade-II: Office of the Registrar General of India, Ministry of Home Affairs - Pay Level 6. Requires a Bachelor's Degree with Statistics as one of the main subjects.

Statutory Boundary (General Category): Born not earlier than 02 August 1994 and not later than 01 August 2008.

Why Your Current Age Is Not Enough

A fatal error among aspirants is checking their age on the current calendar day. SSC CGL never evaluates age as of today; it anchors every eligibility determination to 01 August 2026.

If you are 27 years old today but turn 28 before 01 August 2026, you will be disqualified for all 18–27 Group C posts under the Unreserved category. Always evaluate eligibility based on the official reckoning coordinate.

Does Age Relaxation Change the Post-Wise Age Limit?

Yes. While post-wise age limits specify the base ceilings for Unreserved candidates, eligible applicants belonging to reserved categories receive statutory upper-age extensions:

  • OBC Candidates: +3 Years added to post limit (e.g. 27 + 3 = 30 for Tax Asst; 30 + 3 = 33 for Inspector).
  • SC / ST Candidates: +5 Years added to post limit (e.g. 27 + 5 = 32 for Tax Asst; 30 + 5 = 35 for Inspector).
  • PwBD Candidates: +10 to +15 Years depending on communal category combination.
  • Ex-Servicemen: Net military service deduction + 3 years statutory grace.

Avoid these seven frequent procedural errors that cause application rejection or post-selection cancellation:

MISTAKE 01
Assuming One Universal Limit

Assuming that qualifying for an Inspector post implies you can also be selected for Tax Assistant or Auditor.

MISTAKE 02
Checking Age Without Post Code

Failing to check whether the post carries an 18–27, 20–30, 18–30, or 18–32 ceiling before submitting preference codes.

MISTAKE 03
Using Today's Age Coordinate

Calculating age based on today's calendar date rather than the statutory coordinate of 01 August 2026.

MISTAKE 04
Ignoring Minimum Entry Age

Candidates aged 18 or 19 applying for ASO posts which mandate a strict minimum completed age of 20 years.

MISTAKE 05
Applying Relaxation Uniformly

Assuming OBC means 33 years for every post, forgetting that for 18–27 posts, the OBC ceiling is 30 years.

MISTAKE 06
Using Unofficial Third-Party Tables

Relying on coaching websites with outdated recruitment rules rather than the official SSC gazette notice.

MISTAKE 07
Neglecting Essential Academic Criteria

Meeting the 18–32 age limit for JSO but lacking the mandatory 60% in 12th Mathematics or Statistics degree.

SSC CGL Post-Wise Age Limit FAQs

Is the SSC CGL age limit the same for every post?
No. SSC CGL is a single combined recruitment examination covering over 30 distinct post cadres across multiple Union ministries. Each post cadre is governed by its own statutory Service Recruitment Rules with distinct age limits: 18–27, 20–30, 18–30, or 18–32 years.
What is the SSC CGL age limit in 2026?
The SSC CGL age limit ranges from a statutory minimum of 18 years (or 20 years for ASO cadres) to a statutory maximum of 27, 30, or 32 years depending on the post, reckoned strictly as of 01 August 2026.
Which SSC CGL posts have an 18–27 age limit?
Group C ministerial and clerical cadres carry the 18–27 age band. Key posts include Tax Assistant (CBDT and CBIC), Auditor (C&AG, CGDA, CGA), Accountant / Junior Accountant, and Upper Division Clerk (UDC) / Senior Administrative Assistant.
Which SSC CGL posts have a 20–30 age limit?
Assistant Section Officer (ASO) cadres in Central Secretariat Service (CSS), Ministry of External Affairs (MEA), Intelligence Bureau (IB), Ministry of Railways, and AFHQ carry the 20–30 age band, requiring candidates to be at least 20 years old on the reckoning date.
Which SSC CGL posts have an 18–30 age limit?
Group B executive and enforcement posts carry the 18–30 age band. Key posts include Inspector of Central Excise, Inspector (Preventive Officer), Inspector (Examiner), Inspector of Income Tax, Assistant Enforcement Officer (ED), and Sub-Inspector in CBI and NIA.
Which SSC CGL posts have an 18–32 age limit?
The Junior Statistical Officer (JSO) in the Ministry of Statistics and Programme Implementation (MoSPI) and Statistical Investigator Grade-II in the Office of the Registrar General of India carry the 18–32 age band, which is the highest base maximum limit in the examination.
Can age relaxation change my post-wise eligibility?
Yes. Category age relaxations (OBC +3, SC/ST +5, PwBD +10) are added directly to the targeted post's maximum limit. For example, for an 18–27 post, an OBC candidate is eligible up to 30 years; for an 18–30 post, up to 33 years.
How do I calculate my SSC CGL age for a particular post?
Calculate your completed years, months, and days between your official Date of Birth (recorded on your 10th certificate) and 01 August 2026. Then compare that completed age with the minimum and maximum boundaries of your targeted post.
Does SSC CGL age depend on date of birth?
Yes. The Staff Selection Commission establishes precise 'Not Earlier Than' and 'Not Later Than' birth date cutoff dates for each age band based on the 01 August 2026 crucial reckoning coordinate.
