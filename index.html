When test days or months are less than birth values, borrow directly from the preceding calendar interval:

Beyond the 7-Year Rule: Real Pet Biology

Biological Pet Age Backed by Epigenetics & AAHA Standards

The popular "multiply by 7" rule was debunked by research led by UC San Diego School of Medicine. Mammals age via DNA methylation: young dogs and cats mature rapidly in early life, and cellular wear accelerates based on physical mass.

  • First Year Surge: A 1-year-old dog or cat already matches the biological maturity of a 15–31 human year old.
  • Breed Size Factor: Giant breeds (e.g. Great Danes) age dramatically faster after age 3 compared to small breeds (e.g. Chihuahuas).
  • AAHA Feline Life Stages: Classifies cats into Kitten, Young Adult, Mature Adult, and Senior.

Mathematical Precision & Edge Cases

In non-leap years, February has only 28 days. In English common law and several US jurisdictions, a person born on February 29 legally attains the next year of age on March 1. In other civil codes (and for birthday celebrations), February 28 is commonly observed. Age Calculator Lab preserves the true February 29 anniversary in leap years and clamps safely to February 28 in common years.
Calculators that divide total days by 365.25 introduce up to a 3-day error because human calendar years and months are irregular (28 to 31 days). Age Calculator Lab counts actual completed calendar years, then completed calendar months, then residual days.
Standard JavaScript new Date('YYYY-MM-DD') can shift dates backward by one day for users in negative UTC time zones (such as the Americas). We normalize all input strings to strict UTC midnight, preventing false date rollover.
