What you get

Your baby's age in weeks

Enter your baby's date of birth and the date to calculate on (today by default). The calculator counts the total days between the two dates and divides by 7, showing the age in weeks to two decimal places, with the calendar age in years, months and days and the count of whole weeks alongside. The decimal part is a part-week: 0.5 of a week is about three and a half days.

◌Method

Total days divided by 7

The day count is exact calendar days, leap days included; dividing by 7 gives completed weeks plus a fraction for the remaining days. Edge case: the figure reaches a whole number on the same weekday as the birth — a baby born on a Tuesday is exactly 8.00 weeks old on a Tuesday eight weeks later.

◎Worked example

Born 6 August 2026, calculated 20 October 2026

The span is 75 days, so the age is 75 ÷ 7 = 10.71 weeks — 10 whole weeks and 5 days, matching the calendar age of 0 years, 2 months, 14 days shown beside it.

⌁Limits

Chronological age only

This is a straight count from the birth date: it does not adjust for a baby born early, so for a premature baby this figure differs from a corrected (adjusted) age counted from the due date. It also works at day resolution — the time of birth is not used, so the week count rolls over on the date, not at the exact hour of birth.

Common questions

Baby Age in Weeks Calculator FAQ

Answers about method, inputs, privacy, boundaries and responsible interpretation.

What does this calculator show?

Your baby's age in weeks to two decimal places, from the total days between the birth date and the date you calculate on. The count of whole weeks and the calendar age in years, months and days are shown alongside, and the decimal part is a part-week: 0.5 of a week is about three and a half days.

How many weeks old is a baby born on 6 August 2026, calculated on 20 October 2026?

10.71 weeks. The span is 75 days, and 75 divided by 7 is 10.71 - 10 whole weeks and 5 days - matching the calendar age of 0 years, 2 months and 14 days shown beside it.

How is the age in weeks counted?

The day count is exact calendar days, leap days included, and dividing by 7 gives completed weeks plus a fraction for the remaining days. The figure reaches a whole number on the same weekday as the birth: a baby born on a Tuesday is exactly 8.00 weeks old on a Tuesday eight weeks later.

Does it adjust for a baby born early?

No. This is a straight chronological count from the birth date, so for a premature baby the figure differs from a corrected (adjusted) age counted from the due date. Use the corrected age calculator if you need the due-date-based figure.

Does the time of birth matter?

No. The calculator works at day resolution - the time of birth is not used - so the week count rolls over on the date, at the start of the day, rather than at the exact hour of birth.
