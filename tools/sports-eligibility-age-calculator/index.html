Worked example

Worked example

A player born on 23 November 2015, cutoff 1 September 2026, age range 9 to 11. Enter those values above and the calculator returns the same verdict.

  1. Completed years on 1 September 2026: 10. The 10th birthday was 23 November 2025; the 11th is 23 November 2026, after the cutoff.
  2. Full age on the cutoff: 10 years, 9 months and 9 days.
  3. 10 falls inside the range 9 to 11.

Result: Within the entered age range. Age on 1 September 2026: 10 years, 9 months and 9 days.

Method

How it is calculated

Age = the calendar difference from the birth date to the cutoff date, in UTC, date-only. Only completed years are compared with the range; the months and days are shown for context.

  • Completed years decide. A player who is 10 years and 11 months old counts as 10.
  • One day matters. A player born the day after the cutoff anniversary is a full year younger on the cutoff.
  • 29 February birthdays are clamped to 28 February in non-leap years, the same rule the calendar engine uses everywhere.
  • The league’s rules are not stored. The cutoff date and the age range come from you, so check them with the league.
  • The default cutoff in the form is today’s date, filled in live. Change it to your league’s cutoff before calculating.
Reading your result

Reading your result

The calculator returns the verdict and the age behind it.

  • Verdict: Within the entered age range, or Outside it. This is the primary answer.
  • Age on cutoff: The exact age in years, months and days on the cutoff date.
  • Minimum and maximum: Echoes of the range you entered. Check them first if the verdict surprises you.
  • Cutoff date: Echo of the date the age was judged on.

Limits

What this tool does not do

  • It knows no league’s cutoff date or rules. Those come from you, and must be checked with the league.
  • Completed years only decide. Months and days are shown, but they do not change the verdict.
  • Whole-day dates only. Times of day are ignored.
  • It does not check documents, registration deadlines or any other eligibility rule.
Common questions

Sports Eligibility Age Calculator FAQ

Answers about method, inputs, privacy, boundaries and responsible interpretation.

What does the Sports Eligibility Age Calculator check?

The exact age reached on a sports cutoff date — in years, months and days — and whether the completed years fall inside the minimum and maximum ages you enter. The cutoff and the range are yours; the calculator holds no league’s rules.

Why do the completed years decide, not the months and days?

Eligibility is judged on whole years: the calculator counts complete years from the birth date to the cutoff date, then compares that figure with your minimum and maximum. The months and days are shown too, so you can see how close the player is to the next birthday.

What does the worked example give?

For a cutoff of 1 September 2026, a player born on 10 May 2016 is 10 years, 3 months and 22 days old on that date — 10 completed years, so a range of 9 to 11 returns “Within the entered age range”.

Can one day change the result?

Yes. A player born on 2 September 2016 is still 9 on a 1 September 2026 cutoff — one day short of 10 — so a minimum age of 10 excludes them.

Does it know my league’s cutoff date and rules?

No. It only applies the cutoff date and age range you type in; it does not hold any league’s own cutoff dates or rules, so check those separately with the league.
