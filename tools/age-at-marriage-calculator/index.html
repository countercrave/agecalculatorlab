How it works

How to use the Age at Marriage Calculator

Four short actions, in order. Nothing here repeats the lead or the method below.

1

Date of birth

Enter the date of birth exactly as it appears on the birth record. The day, month and year all matter: one day can move the year figure.

2

Marriage date

Enter the wedding or civil partnership date. It must fall on or after the birth date.

3

Select Calculate

The calculator counts completed years first, then completed months, then the remaining days.

4

Read the age

The result shows the age in years, months and days, plus the total days lived and the next birthday.

Worked example

Worked example: married at 31

Born 3 June 1995, married 17 September 2026. The calculator counts completed units, never a rounded estimate.

  1. Completed years: 3 June 1995 to 3 June 2026 is 31 whole years.
  2. Completed months: 3 June 2026 to 3 September 2026 is 3 whole months.
  3. Remaining days: 3 September 2026 to 17 September 2026 is 14 days.
  4. Total days lived: 11,429 days from birth to the wedding day.
  5. Next birthday: 3 June 2027, which falls 259 days after the wedding.

Result: 31 years, 3 months, 14 days.

Calculation method

How the Age at Marriage Calculator works

The engine counts completed calendar units between two dates. Each unit must finish before it is counted.

RuleCompleted years, then completed months, then remaining days
Completed years firstYears run to the most recent birthday on or before the marriage date. A birthday not yet reached does not count.
Calendar monthsMonths are whole calendar months from that birthday. Short months are handled by the calendar: a 31 January birth counts to 28 February in a short month.
Remaining daysDays left after the last whole month are counted one by one. No month is assumed to be 30 days.
Date-only, UTCDates are treated as date-only values in UTC. The time of the ceremony and time zones make no difference.
Reading your result

What each part of the result means

Each row of the result answers a different question about the wedding-day age.

  • Years, months, days: the primary answer. Only completed units are counted, so the years figure changes on the birthday itself.
  • Total days: the raw day count behind the calendar age. In the example it is 11,429 days.
  • Whole weeks: the total days divided by 7, rounded down.
  • Next birthday: the first birthday after the marriage date, with the days until it. In the example it is 3 June 2027.
  • Born on: the weekday of the birth date.

Limits

What this tool does not do

Genuine limits only. If a limit matters for your decision, check it with the responsible authority.

  • It does not check legal minimum marriage ages. Those rules differ by place and change over time, so check them separately.
  • It does not check whether a marriage is valid. It only counts the age on the date entered.
  • It works to the day only. The time of the ceremony makes no difference to the result.
  • The marriage date must fall on or after the birth date. An earlier date is rejected.
Common questions

Age at Marriage Calculator FAQ

Answers about method, inputs, privacy, boundaries and responsible interpretation.

How is the age on my wedding day calculated?

In completed calendar units: whole years first, counted to the most recent birthday on or before the wedding date, then whole months from there, then the remaining days. Born 3 June 1995 and married 17 September 2026, the age is 31 years, 3 months and 14 days.

What age was I if I married on my birthday?

Exactly that birthday's age, with nothing left over. Someone born on 3 June 1995 who married on 3 June 2026 was exactly 31 years, 0 months and 0 days old on the day.

Can one day really change the answer?

Yes. A wedding on 16 September 2026 gives 31 years, 3 months and 13 days, and 17 September gives 31 years, 3 months and 14 days. A wedding one day before the 31st birthday, on 2 June 2026, would read 30 years, 11 months and 30 days, because the birthday had not yet been reached.

How is this different from the Age at Event Calculator?

The arithmetic is the same completed-unit count, but this page is framed for a wedding or civil partnership date, with the marriage edge cases explained. The Age at Event Calculator answers the same question for any event date, such as a graduation or a job start.

Does the calculator check whether I was legally old enough to marry?

No. The result is an arithmetic answer only. The calculator does not check legal minimum ages or any other eligibility rule, so check the rules for the place of marriage separately if that matters to you.
