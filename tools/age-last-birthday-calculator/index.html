Core Chronological Concept

What Is Age at Last Birthday (ALB)?

Age at Last Birthday (ALB)—often termed completed age or attained age—is the exact number of full, unbroken calendar years that have elapsed between an individual's birth date and a specific reference date. Under this definition, an individual remains at their completed age for all 365 days (or 366 in a leap year) until the stroke of midnight on their next birthday anniversary.

For example, if you were born on June 15, 1990, you attain age 35 on June 15, 2025. You remain legally and chronologically 35 years old throughout July, December, and the following May—all the way until June 14, 2026. Only on June 15, 2026, does your Age at Last Birthday increment to 36.

The Universal Legal & Administrative Benchmark

Around the globe, every major legal statute, constitution, civil registry, driving licensing authority, passport bureau, and government exam board relies strictly on Age at Last Birthday. Whether determining eligibility to vote at 18, purchase alcohol at 21, or apply for civil service posts (such as the SSC CGL Age Calculator), the law requires you to have fully completed the prescribed years. Fractional advancement or actuarial rounding is never recognized in civil law.

Comparative Standards

ALB vs. Age Nearest Birthday (ANB) vs. Age Next Birthday

In commerce and financial services, institutions choose between three distinct age-reckoning standards. Understanding which method governs your contract is crucial when comparing policy quotes and eligibility windows.

Reckoning Standard Mathematical Definition Where It Is Used Annual Transition Date Example (Born Jan 1, Evaluated Oct 1)
Age at Last Birthday (ALB) Completed integer years. Truncates fractional years ($\lfloor \text{Age} \rfloor$). Civil Law, Voting, Passports, Driving Licenses, UK/EU Life Insurance, India (LIC / IRDAI), SSC CGL Exams. On the exact birthday anniversary date. Completed Age X (Remains age X until Dec 31 midnight).
Age Nearest Birthday (ANB) Rounds to the closest birthday anniversary. Steps up at the 6-month midpoint. United States & Canadian Life Insurance (Term, Whole Life, Universal Life), Annuity Pricing, Actuarial Studies. At the exact half-year midpoint (182.5 or 183 days after birthday). Rated as Age X + 1 (October 1 is closer to the upcoming Jan 1).
Age at Next Birthday The upcoming age you will reach, assigned immediately after your birthday. Specialized UK funeral policies, niche endowment plans, traditional credit life products. Immediately on the day following your previous birthday. Rated as Age X + 1 (Assigned next age for entire 12-month period).

As the table illustrates, an applicant evaluated late in their personal year will be classified as one year younger under an ALB policy than under an ANB or Next Birthday policy. For more on the actuarial midpoint method, see our dedicated Age Nearest Birthday Calculator.

Calendar Arithmetic

Calculation Methodology & Mathematical Logic

Completed age is not a decimal approximation created by dividing days by 365.25. It is a discrete Gregorian calendar interval that respects variable month lengths and leap years.

Completed Whole-Year Algorithm Gregorian Calendar Subtraction

Given birth date (Y_dob, M_dob, D_dob) and reference date (Y_ref, M_ref, D_ref):

1. Preliminary Year Difference: ΔY = Y_ref - Y_dob 2. Anniversary Attainment Check: If (M_ref < M_dob) OR (M_ref == M_dob AND D_ref < D_dob): Completed ALB = ΔY - 1 Else: Completed ALB = ΔY 3. Last Birthday Date: D_last = Birthday anniversary in year (Y_dob + ALB) 4. Next Birthday Date: D_next = Birthday anniversary in year (Y_dob + ALB + 1)

February 29 Leap Day Birthdays ("Leaplings")

For individuals born on February 29 during a leap year, non-leap years do not contain a February 29. Different legal regimes handle this edge case in one of two ways:

  • Common Law Standard (UK, US, India): Under English common law and Supreme Court precedents, an individual completes a year of life on the first day of the following calendar period. Because February 28 is still part of the 365th day, the birthday anniversary legally concludes on March 1 in common years.
  • Civil Law Standard (Certain EU jurisdictions, New Zealand): Recognizes February 28 as the last day of February, assigning the completed milestone on the final day of the birth month.

Our calculator seamlessly tracks the exact calendar days elapsed, ensuring complete transparency for either interpretation.

Institutional Applications

Legal, Regulatory & Official Uses of ALB

While financial underwriting sometimes experiments with midpoint rounding, the overwhelming majority of institutional records require Age at Last Birthday.

1. Civil Law & Legal Majority

Every statutory right tied to chronological maturity requires completed years:

  • Voting Rights (Age 18): You must have celebrated your 18th birthday on or before the registration qualifying cutoff date. (See our Voting Age Date Guide).
  • Motor Vehicle Licensing: Learner permits (16) and commercial driver licenses (18 or 21) require full completed years.
  • Contractual Capacity: Entering binding financial contracts, mortgages, or bank agreements requires reaching legal majority (18 completed years).

2. Competitive Government Examinations (SSC, UPSC, Banking)

In recruitment notifications issued by the Staff Selection Commission, Union Public Service Commission, and State PSCs, candidates are evaluated against a crucial reckoning date (such as 1 August 2026). The Commission strictly computes Age at Last Birthday. For example, if a post requires candidates to be between 18 and 27 years old, an applicant who is 27 years and 1 day old on the crucial date has exceeded the completed age limit and is ineligible (unless qualifying for statutory category relaxations).

3. UK, European & Commonwealth Life Insurance

In the United Kingdom, Europe, Australia, and New Zealand, statutory consumer protection regulations require life insurance quotes to be based on Age at Last Birthday. Insurers are prohibited from arbitrarily jumping an applicant to an older rate band six months early. Similarly, in India, the Life Insurance Corporation (LIC) and Insurance Regulatory and Development Authority of India (IRDAI) mandate Age at Last Birthday for consumer policy underwriting.

Common questions

Age at Last Birthday FAQ

Authoritative answers on completed age, legal majority, insurance underwriting, and anniversary rules.

What is Age at Last Birthday (ALB)?

Age at Last Birthday (ALB)—also known as completed age—is the number of complete whole years an individual has lived as of their most recent birthday anniversary. A person remains at their completed age until midnight on the day before their next birthday.

Why is Age at Last Birthday considered the universal legal standard?

Civil codes, government statutes, and international standards (including ISO 8601 interval conventions) define legal majority, voting eligibility, driver licensing, and criminal responsibility based on completed whole years. Under the law, you do not attain a new age until you have completed all 365 (or 366) days of the preceding year.

How does ALB differ from Age Nearest Birthday (ANB)?

ALB maintains your age at the completed integer until your exact birthday anniversary arrives. ANB rounds your age up to the next birthday once you cross the half-year midpoint (about 183 days after your birthday), meaning you become one year older in insurance rating tables six months before your actual birthday.

Do insurance companies use Age at Last Birthday?

Yes. Life insurers in the United Kingdom, European Union, Australia, and India (mandated by LIC and IRDAI regulations) predominantly use Age at Last Birthday (ALB). In contrast, US and Canadian insurers often use Age Nearest Birthday (ANB).

Which age convention do government competitive exams like SSC CGL and UPSC use?

Government recruitment examinations strictly require completed chronological age (Age at Last Birthday) as of the crucial cutoff date specified in the official notification (such as 1 August 2026 for SSC CGL). Actuarial rounding (ANB) is never permitted.

How does ALB handle February 29 leap day birthdays?

For leaplings born on February 29, the completed anniversary in common (non-leap) years is determined by legal jurisdiction: English common law, India, and most US states treat March 1 as the completion date of the year, while civil jurisdictions (such as New Zealand or Taiwan) recognize February 28.

Is my age on my passport and driver's license ALB?

Yes. All civil identification documents—including passports, driver's licenses, national identity cards, and birth certificates—record and reckon age strictly based on completed years (Age at Last Birthday).

Can my Age at Last Birthday ever decrease?

No. Completed age is a monotonically increasing step function. It stays constant throughout the 365 (or 366) days between birthdays and steps up by exactly 1 on your birthday anniversary.

Does this calculator require sign-up or store my date of birth?

No. The calculation runs 100% locally on your device in your web browser. No personal data, birth dates, or calculation queries are sent to any server or third party.

What is the difference between ALB and running age?

Running age refers to the year of life you are currently living in (e.g. 'I am in my 36th year'). Age at Last Birthday refers to the years you have already completed (e.g. 'I am 35 years completed'). Running age is always equal to ALB + 1.
