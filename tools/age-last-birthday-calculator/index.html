Core Chronological Concept

What Is Age at Last Birthday?

Age at last birthday (ALB) is the number of complete, elapsed calendar years a person has lived as of a given reference date. It counts full birthday anniversaries already reached, ignoring any fraction of the current year in progress.

For example, someone born on November 18, 1994, is strictly 31 years old on October 10, 2026. Even though they are merely 39 days away from celebrating their 32nd anniversary, their completed chronological age remains 31 until the calendar reaches November 18.

ALB is commonly termed completed age or attained age. It contrasts directly with Age at Next Birthday (which anticipates the upcoming milestone) and Age Nearest Birthday (which rounds upward once the 6-month midpoint is passed).

Universal Legal & Statutory Standard

Across the globe, civil statutes, passports, driving licenses, voter registrations (detailed in our Voting Age Date Guide), and competitive recruitment exams (such as the SSC CGL Age Calculator) evaluate eligibility based strictly on Age at Last Birthday. Fractional rounding is never recognized under statutory civil law.

Practical Calculation

Worked Example: Age at Last Birthday

Consider a candidate born on November 18, 1994, whose eligibility is evaluated on October 10, 2026:

Evaluating this candidate step-by-step under the Gregorian completed age rule:

  • Calendar Year Difference: 2026 − 1994 = 32 years.
  • Anniversary Comparison: The evaluation date is October 10. The candidate's birthday falls on November 18. Because October comes before November, the 2026 birthday has not yet occurred.
  • Deduction Rule: Since the 32nd birthday has not arrived, subtract 1: 32 − 1 = 31 years.
  • Milestone Proximity: Even though the candidate is only 39 days away from turning 32, their legal and completed age remains 31.

Under common speech, someone might say they are "almost 32," but on any official document or application form, their completed age is strictly 31.

Common questions

Frequently Asked Questions

Direct answers to essential questions about completed age and birthday reckoning.

What does age at last birthday mean?

It means the number of complete years reached by the selected reference date. A person who has celebrated 31 birthdays has a completed age of 31.

How do I calculate completed age?

Subtract your birth year from the reference year. If your birthday has not yet occurred in that reference year, subtract one from the result. Otherwise, completed age is simply reference year minus birth year.

Does age at last birthday change on January 1?

Not necessarily. It changes on the person's birthday anniversary, not automatically at the start of a new calendar year.

What if my birthday has not happened yet this year?

Your completed age remains one less than the difference between the reference year and your birth year until your birthday arrives.

What is the difference between completed age and exact age?

Completed age counts whole years only. Exact age can also include the remaining calendar months and days.

How is age at last birthday different from age at next birthday?

Age at last birthday is the age already reached. Age at next birthday is the age you will reach on your upcoming birthday.

Can I calculate my age on a past date?

Yes. Enter your date of birth and the historical reference date you want to check.

Does this calculator calculate age at nearest birthday?

No. Age at last birthday counts completed whole years. Age at nearest birthday follows a different convention and should be calculated with our dedicated Age Nearest Birthday Calculator.
