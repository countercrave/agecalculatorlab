What you get

How the days left in the year are counted

Enter any date. The calculator works out which day of the year it is — counting January 1 as day 1 — and counts the days from that date to December 31. Alongside the days remaining, it reports the weekday, the ISO week number and whether the year is a leap year.

▦Method

Day-of-year arithmetic

The count is pure calendar arithmetic. The date’s position in the year is found by counting days forward from January 1, and the days left are the days from your date to December 31 — 365 days in an ordinary year, 366 in a leap year. No time of day is involved, so the answer is the same in every time zone.

◎Example

Worked example: 20 October 2026

October 20, 2026 is day 293 of 2026, a Tuesday in ISO week 43. Counting from that date to December 31 leaves 72 days. Enter 2026-10-20 in the calculator above and it returns the same figures.

⌁Limits

Year-end and leap-year edges

On December 31 the days left read 0, and on January 1 they read 364 — or 365 in a leap year. Leap years shift every date after February by one day: February 29, 2028 is day 60 of its year, with 306 days left. The count covers calendar days only, not working days.

Common questions

Days Left in Year Calculator FAQ

Answers about method, inputs, privacy, boundaries and responsible interpretation.

How are the days left in the year counted?

The calculator first finds which day of the year your date is, counting 1 January as day 1, then counts the days from that date to 31 December. Alongside the days remaining, it reports the weekday, the ISO week number and whether the year is a leap year.

How many days are left in the year on 20 October 2026?

72 days. 20 October 2026 is day 293 of 2026, a Tuesday in ISO week 43, and counting from that date to 31 December leaves 72 days.

How many days are left on 1 January?

364 days in an ordinary year, or 365 in a leap year. On 31 December the count reads 0, because the year ends that day.

Does a leap year change the answer?

Yes, for dates after February: the extra day shifts every later date one day further through the year. 29 February 2028 is day 60 of its year, with 306 days left after it.

Does the time of day or my time zone matter?

No. The count is pure calendar arithmetic at day resolution, with no time of day involved, so the answer is the same in every time zone. It covers calendar days only, not working days.
