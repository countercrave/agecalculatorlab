How it works

Get your Subtract Months From Date result in four clear steps

Enter the starting date and how many months to move back, then select Calculate. The explanation is broken into short actions so you can understand the workflow without reading a wall of text.

2

Number of months

What you get

How the date moves back by months

Enter a starting date and a number of months. The calculator moves the date back by that many whole calendar months, keeping the same day of the month where the target month has one and clamping to its last day where it does not, and shows the resulting date with its weekday.

▦Method

Calendar months, month-end protected

The shift is made on the calendar rather than in days: the month is stepped back the requested number of times and the day number is preserved whenever possible. When the target month is shorter — moving back from the 31st into a 30-day month or February — the day clamps to that month's last day instead of spilling into the next month.

◎Worked example

Example: 6 months before 20 October 2026

Six months before 20 October 2026 is 20 April 2026. Edge case: one month before 31 March 2026 is 28 February 2026 — not 3 March — because February 2026 has only 28 days and the date clamps to its last day.

⌁Limits

Months are not a fixed number of days

Calendar months differ in length, so the same month count removes different numbers of days depending on which months are crossed — subtracting 3 months spans between 89 and 92 days. If you need an exact day count instead, subtract days directly.

Common questions

Subtract Months From Date Calculator FAQ

Answers about method, inputs, privacy, boundaries and responsible interpretation.

What happens when I subtract a month from the 31st?

The day number is preserved where the earlier month has one and clamped to its last day where it does not. One month before 31 March 2026 is 28 February 2026 — not 3 March — because February 2026 has only 28 days.

What date is 6 months before 20 October 2026?

20 April 2026. The calculator steps the month back six places on the calendar — September, August, July, June, May, April — and the day stays on the 20th because April has a 20th.

Why doesn't subtracting 3 months always remove the same number of days?

Because calendar months have different lengths, so the same month count spans different day totals depending on which months it crosses — between 89 and 92 days for three months. Three months before 20 October 2026 is 20 July 2026, a span of 92 days.

Is subtracting months the same as subtracting 30 days per month?

No. Subtracting 3 months from 20 October 2026 gives 20 July 2026, while subtracting 90 days gives 22 July 2026. The month method keeps the day of the month anchored; the day method counts exact days, so the two answers drift apart whenever a 31-day month is crossed.

Can I subtract more than a year of months in one calculation?

Yes. The count rolls back across year boundaries without splitting, so 14 months before 20 October 2026 is 20 August 2025. Enter the full month count and the calculator handles the year change for you.
