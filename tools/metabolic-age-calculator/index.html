How it works

Get your Metabolic Age result in four clear steps

Enter your age and lifestyle inputs, then select Calculate. The explanation is broken into short actions so you can understand the workflow without reading a wall of text.

1

Chronological age

Enter your age in completed years. This is the starting figure that every lifestyle adjustment below is added to or subtracted from.

2

Average sleep hours

Enter your average sleep per night in hours, then the remaining answers: Active days per week, Resting heart rate (optional — it can be left blank) and Smoking status — No, Former or Current.

3

Select Calculate

Each answer scores fixed whole years: sleeping 7 to 9 hours subtracts 1, while under 6 or over 9 hours adds 2; five or more active days subtract 2 and one or fewer adds 2; a resting heart rate under 65 subtracts 1 and over 90 adds 2; smoking adds 4 years, or 1 for a former smoker. Exactly 6 hours of sleep sits between the bands and scores 0.

4

Review, copy or print

Read the estimate next to the adjustment total: age 40, sleeping 8 hours, active 5 days, a resting heart rate of 60 and not smoking scores −4, so the estimate is 36 years — the same person sleeping 6 hours would read 37. It is an educational estimate that never goes below 13 years, not a medical measurement of your body.

What you get

An educational estimate from age and lifestyle inputs

Enter your chronological age, average sleep hours, active days per week, resting heart rate (optional) and smoking status. The calculator applies a fixed set of small year adjustments for each input and adds them to your age, giving an educational estimate shown alongside the adjustment total and the values you entered. It is a rough comparison built from general lifestyle factors, not a medical measurement of your body.

♡Method

Fixed adjustments, added to your age

Sleep of 7 to 9 hours subtracts 1 year, while under 6 or over 9 hours adds 2; five or more active days subtract 2 and one or fewer adds 2; a resting heart rate under 65 subtracts 1 and over 90 adds 2; smoking adds 4 and former smoking adds 1. Edge case: exactly 6 hours of sleep sits between the bands and scores 0, and the final estimate never goes below 13 years.

◎Worked example

Age 40, sleeping 8 hours, active 5 days a week

Sleep of 8 hours scores −1, activity of 5 days scores −2, a resting heart rate of 60 scores −1 and not smoking scores 0 — a total adjustment of −4 years. The estimate is 40 − 4 = 36 years. The same person sleeping 6 hours instead would score 0 for sleep and read 37 years.

⌁Limits

An estimate, not a measurement

The adjustments are broad educational rules applied to a handful of self-reported inputs; they are not based on blood tests, body composition or any clinical measure, and small input changes move the result by whole years. Treat the number as a prompt to think about sleep, activity and habits — not as a diagnosis or a medical age, and not a substitute for professional advice.

Common questions

Metabolic Age Calculator FAQ

Answers about method, inputs, privacy, boundaries and responsible interpretation.

How is the metabolic age estimate calculated?

A fixed set of small year adjustments is applied to your chronological age, one for each input. Sleep of 7 to 9 hours subtracts 1 year, while under 6 or over 9 hours adds 2. Five or more active days subtract 2, and 1 day or fewer adds 2. A resting heart rate under 65 subtracts 1 and over 90 adds 2. Smoking adds 4 years, or 1 for a former smoker.

What estimate does a 40-year-old with strong habits get?

Sleeping 8 hours (-1), being active 5 days a week (-2), a resting heart rate of 60 (-1) and not smoking (0) gives a total adjustment of -4 years, so a 40-year-old reads 36 years. The estimate is shown alongside the adjustment total and the values entered.

Why does sleeping exactly 6 hours score 0?

Exactly 6 hours sits between the sleep bands: 7 to 9 hours scores -1, and under 6 or over 9 hours scores +2, so 6 hours scores 0. In the worked example on this page, the same person reads 37 years instead of 36 with 6 hours of sleep.

What is the lowest estimate the calculator will show?

The final estimate never goes below 13 years, however favourable the adjustments are.

Is this a measurement of my metabolism?

No. The adjustments are broad educational rules applied to a handful of self-reported inputs; they are not based on blood tests, body composition or any clinical measure. Treat the number as a prompt to think about sleep, activity and habits, not as a diagnosis or a medical age, and not a substitute for professional advice.
