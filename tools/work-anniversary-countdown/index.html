Calculation method

Work Anniversary Countdown calculates the next employment anniversary and completed service duration using Gregorian calendar arithmetic. Dates are treated as date-only values, avoiding accidental time-of-day and daylight-saving shifts.

Important: Weekend, holiday, legal, medical, financial, school or workplace policies are included only when the tool explicitly asks for them. Confirm official decisions with the responsible authority.
